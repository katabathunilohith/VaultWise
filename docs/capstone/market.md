# Who needs a payout plan?

**First audience to test.** Food and grocery riders in Vijayawada and Guntur who pay for their own fuel, expect a platform payout and have a household bill due around the same time. Ask how often this happened in the last four weeks and what it cost in time, borrowing or deferred purchases. Local figures are unknown.

## What people reported

| Evidence | Implication for the prototype |
|---|---|
| **App review.** On 16 November 2026, Zomato reviewer Ganesh described paying for petrol before knowing the trip earnings. Zomato advertises daily or weekly payouts and instant withdrawal. [Google Play listing and reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en-GB&id=com.zomato.delivery) | Plan from cash in hand and the rider’s payout date. Ask whether instant withdrawal already solves the problem. |
| **Community account.** A rider whose expected pay went towards deductions wrote, “Let me keep something for fuel.” Another rider reported a payout still missing after three weeks. [Deduction accounts](https://www.reddit.com/r/swiggy/comments/1x0plt0/worked_as_a_delivery_person_for_three_weeks/) · [Delay account](https://www.reddit.com/r/swiggy/comments/1qkgccr/payout_pending_even_floating_cash_not_settled/) | Test a disrupted payout before committing cash. |
| **Published fieldwork.** Dvara’s 14-day diaries with 22 Bengaluru platform workers in 2022 documented cash earmarked for fuel, food and instalments. IDinsight surveyed 2,547 delivery drivers on one Indian platform. Expenses averaged 32% of gross earnings in its subgroup working over eight hours a day on full-time-like schedules, mainly fuel and maintenance. [Dvara, especially §2.3 and Appendix 7](https://dvararesearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Research-Report_-The-financial-lives-of-platform-workers-A-diaries-study-in-Bengaluru-India.pdf) · [IDinsight study summary](https://www.idinsight.org/article/the-changing-landscape-of-work-insights-into-indias-delivery-platform-gig-workforce/) | Combine household bills and work expenses. Ask each rider for actual costs. |

## What the rider can use today

| Alternative | Cost or effort today | Reason to try Vaultwise, to test in person |
|---|---|---|
| Cash set aside at home or in a bank balance | No extra software fee. The rider keeps track of which part covers each commitment. [Dvara field study](https://dvararesearch.com/the-financial-lives-of-platform-workers-a-diaries-study-in-bengaluru-india/) | See the first uncovered expense when the payout date changes. |
| Giga rider income tracker | Logging orders, incentives and fuel buys produces net earnings per shift. The core tracker works offline without an account. [Developer listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.neuraitech.giga) | Answer whether upcoming fuel and a household bill can both be covered from money already on hand. |
| Kosh household budget | Free open-source Android app. It already calculates daily spending until payday after upcoming bills and supports a salary cycle. [Project documentation](https://github.com/AIM-IT4/kosh) | Compare the expected date with a delay from personal history. Test whether reusing the longest delay from up to six actual payout cycles is worth returning for. |
| Platform payout screen | The worker already uses it. Reviewing payouts takes time, and cash-out terms can vary. [Zomato partner listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en-GB&id=com.zomato.delivery) | Keep the platform for settlement and try Vaultwise for the household decision across income sources. |

**Commercial test.** Recruit off-shift through a local rider collective. Ask a collective or welfare sponsor who approves a pilot budget and what outcome would justify renewal. Measure repeat planning next payout cycle. Sponsor interest, retention and revenue are unmeasured.

**Limits of this evidence.** Sources selected for relevance, checked 27 November 2026. Store reviews rotate, community writers self-select and payout disputes are unverified. The studies sampled different groups. Local incidence, switching and willingness to pay require fieldwork.
